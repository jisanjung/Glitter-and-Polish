## Vision & positioning

Inferred from the live site copy and the 2026 redesign:

- **Promise:** "Your nails are *the reflection* of yourself." Premium care, done with care.
- **Pillars:** premium products (organic spa options, long-wear polish) · trained,
  friendly technicians · clean & sanitized (tools cleaned for every guest) · everyone
  welcome, including a kids' menu (9 & under).
- **Feel:** a calm, clean, pristine neighborhood spa where you can unwind after work.
  Modern and polished, never loud or salesy.
- **Honesty:** "We don't cut corners. Your feedback shapes how we work."
- **Conversion goal:** make booking effortless — "Book online in under a minute" — while
  keeping phone booking first-class (same-day and group bookings are easiest by phone).

### What this means for new features

- Booking must stay fast and simple on a phone: few steps, big tap targets, clear times.
- Choosing a technician should feel personal (names, optionally a photo/specialty), with a
  "No preference / first available" default so it never slows people down.
- Emails (confirmations) should sound like the site: warm, short, with date, time,
  service, technician, address, phone, and how to change/cancel.
- The admin page is a tool for the front desk: speed and clarity over decoration, usable
  on a tablet/phone at the desk.
- Photos should show real work from the salon and the real space; the gallery is a key
  trust signal for a nail business.
